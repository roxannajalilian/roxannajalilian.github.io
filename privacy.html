Privacy & Concerns

This app stores information only in your browser (localStorage) so it can remember your quiz score and access verification. It does not send your messages anywhere.

What we store

  • Your age (to verify 18+ access)
  • Your last quiz score + answers
  • Optional analyzer results (not your files)

If something is unsafe

If you’re in danger or feel unsafe, talk to a trusted adult or local emergency services.
