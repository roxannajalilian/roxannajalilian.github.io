Privacy + Terms

This is a school project-style web app. It stores some info only on your device so the app can work.

What we store

• Your age (only to verify 18+ access)
• Your latest quiz score + answers (to build the Plan)
• Optional analysis results (if you use the analyzer)

Where it’s stored

Everything is stored in localStorage on your browser/device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

18+ only

If you enter an age under 18, you’ll be blocked and redirected back to Start.

Safety note

This app can’t provide emergency help. If you’re in danger or feel unsafe, contact local emergency services or someone you trust.
