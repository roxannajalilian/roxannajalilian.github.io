Your Privacy

Delulu Detector does not collect, sell, or share your personal information. All responses are stored only on your device using local storage.

What we store

Nothing is sent to a server.

This app is not a replacement for professional mental health support. If a user appears to be in distress, they may be redirected to safer resources.

If you or someone you know needs help, consider reaching out to a trusted adult or a professional support service.

Age restriction

This app is intended for users aged 18 and older. Users under 18 will be blocked and redirected.
