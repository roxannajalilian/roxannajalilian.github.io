Terms of Use

  • This app is for educational / entertainment guidance only.
  • It does not replace professional advice (counselling/therapy/legal advice).
  • Do not enter private information you don’t want saved on your device.
  • If content suggests harm or danger, the app will recommend real support resources.

Privacy

  • No server is used — everything runs in your browser.
  • If you choose to save results, they are stored locally using LocalStorage.
  • You can delete saved results anytime using “Delete all”.

Age & Safety

  • Under 13: locked out (youth safety).
  • 13–17: teen-safe mode (less explicit questions, calmer advice).
  • 18+: adult mode (more direct questions).

If you are in immediate danger or feel unsafe, contact local emergency services.
