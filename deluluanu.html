Delulu Detector

This app offers a PG, fun quiz for minors (14–17) — no AI scanning of messages. Adults (18+) may use AI-assisted analysis (text/voice) and advanced tools.

By clicking Accept you confirm you read the privacy note. Adults will be asked to consent before analysis.

Privacy & Terms

Minors (14–17)

  • Only PG quiz. No screenshots, no message scanning, no voice processing.
  • Answers are kept locally (not sent anywhere).

Adults (18+)

  • Advanced features (text/voice) are processed locally in-browser where possible.
  • If back-end is later connected, screenshots/voice would be encrypted and auto-deleted after analysis (opt-in).

General

  • Rules: honest DOB; suspicious/contradictory inputs may trigger temporary or permanent lockout for safety.
  • Appeals may be submitted from the Appeal page.
