Privacy + Terms

This is a school/demo web app. It gives general “delulu” insights (not professional advice).

Privacy

• Your text/results stay on your device.
• No accounts. No server. Nothing is uploaded.
• We only use localStorage so the app can remember your quiz/plan.

What we store

• Age verification (18+ access)
• Last quiz score + answers (to build your plan)
• Optional analysis result (if you analyze text)

18+ Rule

You must be 18 or older to use Menu, Quiz, Plan, Analysis, and Game. If you enter an age under 18, you’ll be redirected to the blocked page.

Safety note

This app can’t help with emergencies. If you feel unsafe, contact local emergency services or talk to a trusted person.
